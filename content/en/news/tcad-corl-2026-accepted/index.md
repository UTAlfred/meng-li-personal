The title of the accepted TCAD paper is "HDA-MoE: Hybrid Parallelism and Dynamic, Adaptive Scheduling for Mixture-of-Experts with 3D Near-Memory Processing", featuring a novel scheduling algorithm for MoE inference on 3D DRAM accelerators. This is a journal extension of our HD-MoE paper accepted by ICCAD'2025. The work is led by our PhD student Haochen Huang.

The title of the accepted CoRL paper is "Jetson-PI: Towards Onboard Real-Time Robot Control via Foresight-Aligned Asynchronous Inference", featuring asynchronous inference optimization and edge GPU deployment optimization. The work is led by our PhD student Zebin Yang.
