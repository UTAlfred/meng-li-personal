# Summary for listings and search engines
summary: Team SEC, formed by PhD students [Qingyu Guo](/authors/qingyu_guo_2020g/), [Linfeng Zhong](/authors/linfeng_zhong_2026g/), [Songqiang Xu](/authors/songqiang_xu_2023g/), and [Renjie Wei](/authors/renjie_wei_2022g/) has won the 1st Place in AICAS Grand Challenge on LLM System Design. Prof. Yuan Wang and I are the supervisors for the team. In the contest, Team SEC successfully deploys a Qwen-0.5B LLM on a lightweight KV260 FPGA and achieves 35 tokens/s decoding throughput, 4 times higher than the second place.

Team SEC, formed by PhD students [Qingyu Guo]({{< relref "/authors/qingyu_guo_2020g" >}}), [Linfeng Zhong]({{< relref "/authors/linfeng_zhong_2026g" >}}), [Songqiang Xu]({{< relref "/authors/songqiang_xu_2023g" >}}), and [Renjie Wei]({{< relref "/authors/renjie_wei_2022g" >}}) has won the 1st Place in AICAS Grand Challenge on LLM System Design. Prof. Yuan Wang and I are the supervisors for the team. In the contest, Team SEC successfully deploys a Qwen-0.5B LLM on a lightweight KV260 FPGA and achieves 35 tokens/s decoding throughput, 4 times higher than the second place.
